# When Teams Move at Different Speeds

In an existing system,
not all teams progress at the same pace.

And that's not a malfunction.
It's a natural state.

## The Illusion: A Healthy System Moves at a Uniform Pace

It's easy to want:

- All teams to adopt the same standard
- Everyone to go through change together
- And every component to align to the same level of maturity

But in live systems,
this almost never happens.

Because different teams:

- Are under different pressures
- Serve different users
- And live with different legacy debts

An uneven pace isn't a sign of management failure -
it's a result of reality.

## Why Asymmetry Always Forms

Asymmetry between teams is born when:

- One team is in a maintenance phase, another in growth
- One service is stable, another is still taking shape
- Part of the system gets "touched every day," while another barely changes

The problem isn't the difference itself -
it's trying to erase it by force.

## Why Forced Standardization Fails

When you try to force alignment,
one of two things happens:

- Weaker teams get crushed
- Stronger teams slow down to "not stand out"

In both cases,
the system pays a price.

Standardization that doesn't account for context
creates compliance on paper -
and friction in practice.

## Coordination Without Uniformity

The solution isn't full uniformity,
but conscious coordination.

Coordination means:

- Agreement on clear boundaries
- Explicit expectations between teams
- And understanding where it's okay to be different

Not every component needs to be identical.
But you do need to know:

- What it relies on
- And what relies on it

## Where Responsible Engineers Come In

Responsible engineers don't ask:
"how does everyone work the same way?"

They ask:

- Where is variance dangerous
- And where does it actually protect the system

They don't try to eliminate gaps -
they manage them.

## The Bottom Line

Different paces aren't a problem to solve.
They're a reality you need to learn to live with.

A stable system isn't one where everyone advances together -
it's one that knows how to contain uneven movement
without falling apart.
